Engineers at Chaincode are tasked with identifying and pursuing the work they believe is most important to Bitcoin's long-term success. We rarely list traditionally defined roles because our work is so self-directed. The best way to apply to work at Chaincode is to do the kind of work you would do at Chaincode: make meaningful contributions to Bitcoin open-source research and development.

We're always interested in ideas and work related to Bitcoin that are not on our radar. If you're working on problems in distributed systems, cryptography, computer security, networking, privacy, or related fields and believe your work has relevance to Bitcoin, we'd love to hear from you. Reach out to us at info@chaincode.com.
