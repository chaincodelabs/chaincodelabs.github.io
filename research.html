Chaincode's research initiatives aim to support rigorous investigation of the open questions and challenges facing Bitcoin. We seek to foster greater engagement between Bitcoin and the academic community, helping ensure that the strongest ideas and insights contribute to Bitcoin's long-term development.

Our team has worked on:

  • Bitcoin Mempool Linearization
  • Unjamming Lightning: A Systematic Approach
  • Nested MuSig2
  • Bitcoin and quantum computing
  • Lightning Network Economics: Channels
  • Bandwidth-Efficient Transaction Relay for Bitcoin

RESEARCH DAY

Bitcoin Research Day brings together researchers and practitioners to share academic work that advances understanding of Bitcoin and its open challenges.

RESEARCH PRIZE

Each year, we award a Research Prize recognizing outstanding research that advances the state of the art in Bitcoin.

BITCOIN SCHOLARSHIP

Our scholarship provides a full year's tuition to exceptional students who are committed to contributing to Bitcoin open source research and development.

PHD INTERNSHIPS

We are happy to host PhD students for summer research internships relevant to our work. See our jobs page for more information.
